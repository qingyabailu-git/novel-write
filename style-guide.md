# Reusable style guide

## Style belongs here

- Viewpoint, tense, and knowledge boundary.
- Voice, emotional distance, and tone.
- Sentence rhythm and paragraph size.
- Dialogue behavior and action-to-dialogue balance.
- Scene openings, transitions, and endings.
- Description density, sensory detail, and metaphor policy.
- Pacing, chapter length, and cliffhanger preference.
- Formatting rules and forbidden habits.
- Content boundaries and acceptable intensity.

## Canon does not belong here

Do not put character biographies, plot events, locations, factions, magic rules, relationship facts, or future chapter plans into a reusable style reference. Put those in lore, outline, chapter groups, or state files.

## Reference extraction

When a user supplies prose as a style reference, summarize recurring techniques instead of storing a long quotation. Prefer observations such as sentence length, scene distance, dialogue density, and how emotion is expressed through action. Keep any source-specific facts out of the result.

## Application check

Before applying a style to a chapter, compare it with CREATOR.md and the user's current request. Project-specific constraints take priority over a generic style reference.
