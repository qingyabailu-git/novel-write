# Lore boundaries

Lore is the stable canon layer. Keep entries small, searchable, and limited to facts that should remain true across many chapters.

## Recommended record

- Type: character, world, location, faction, rule, ability, item, or other.
- Name and aliases.
- Brief description: identity, key facts, use context, and search words.
- Stable facts: concise Markdown sections.
- Keywords: names, aliases, and recurring terms.
- Source: user-confirmed, existing text, or pending inference.
- Status: confirmed, proposed, or deprecated.

## Store in lore

- Identity and durable background.
- Personality and long-term motivations.
- Stable relationships.
- Ability limits and costs.
- World rules, taboos, and institutions.
- Recurring locations and factions.
- Important objects and durable clues.

## Store elsewhere

- Current position, injury, mood, goal, inventory, and immediate knowledge: character-states.md.
- Chapter summaries and recent events: progress.md.
- Planned scenes and future beats: outline.md or chapter-groups/.
- Facts that only exist inside a chapter: chapters/.

When a chapter reveals a stable fact, prepare a small proposed lore patch and ask for confirmation if the change affects future canon.
